How suggestions work

1. Your tasteWe look at what you kept: kinds of places (café, park…), cuisines, tags like “terrace” or “vegan”, and where your faves are.
2. A precise searchEach card shows the exact filter we send to OpenStreetMap, Wikipedia or Wikidata. Nothing runs until you tap.
3. You decideResults you already saved are hidden. Saving one puts it in your inbox, sorted, so it teaches the next search. Top faves count what people save around you, anonymously.
