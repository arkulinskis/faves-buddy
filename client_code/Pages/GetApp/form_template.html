Get the Faves Buddy app

No app store needed: Faves Buddy installs straight from your browser in a few seconds, opens full screen like any other app, and is always the latest version. On Android it also shows up in your share menu, so tabs and screenshots go straight in.

Faves Buddy is already installed on this device. Open it from your home screen.

🤖

Android

Chrome, Samsung Internet, Edge

  1. Open faves-buddy.anvil.app in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Tap Install above, or Chrome's menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install.
  3. Find Faves Buddy on your home screen. To save a pile of tabs: Chrome's tab switcher → Select tabs → ⋮ → Share → Faves Buddy. Screenshots: Share from Photos.
📱

iPhone & iPad

Safari (iOS 16.4 and later: also Chrome and Edge)

  1. Open faves-buddy.anvil.app in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom (iPad: top right).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open it from your home screen. To bring in tabs, use Safari's Copy N Links and paste them in Tidy my open tabs; for screenshots, Sweep my screenshots.
💻

Computer

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc

  1. Chrome or Edge: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (or menu → Save and share → Install).
  2. Add the Chrome extension to save a tab, or a whole window of tabs, in one click.

Why isn't it in the App Store or Google Play?

Faves Buddy is a web app you install from the browser (a “progressive web app”). It's the same app on every device, it updates itself, it takes almost no space, and your faves are the same everywhere you sign in.

Does it work offline?

It needs a connection to save and sort things, because your collection lives in your account. Pictures you choose are shrunk and their text is read on your device.

How do I remove it?

Like any app: touch and hold its icon and choose Remove (iPhone) or Uninstall (Android). Your faves stay in your account.
