Getting your tabs and screenshots in

Faves Buddy is installed on this device.

Android phone (Chrome)
  1. Install Faves Buddy once (the Install button, or Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen). Step by step
  2. In Chrome, open the tab switcher, touch and hold any tab (or tap ⋮) and choose Select tabs.
  3. Tick the tabs (or all of them), tap ⋮ → Share tabs and pick Faves Buddy.
  4. They open here, already sorted. Swipe through them. Close the tabs in Chrome afterwards.

Screenshots: in Photos or Gallery, select several, tap Share and pick Faves Buddy. Or use Sweep my screenshots above.

iPhone or iPad
  1. In Safari, open the tab overview, then touch and hold the tab group's name (or tap ⋯) and choose Copy N Links.
  2. Come back here, open Sort my open tabs and tap Paste. The titles come along.
  3. Chrome on iPhone: in the tab grid tap Edit → Select tabs, pick them, then Share → Copy, and paste here.

Screenshots: Sweep my screenshots lets you pick a batch from the Screenshots album. To keep Faves Buddy on your Home Screen: Safari Share → Add to Home Screen (step by step).

A computer (Chrome extension)
  1. Install the Chrome extension and connect it.
  2. Open it and look under Tabs on your other devices: with Chrome sync on, your phone's open Chrome tabs are listed there.
  3. Tap Sort these and they open here as a pile to swipe through. (Or save this computer's window in one click.)
