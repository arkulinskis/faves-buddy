Faves Buddy for Chrome

Save the tab you're on, a whole window of tabs (and close them), a link, a picture or selected text, from the toolbar or the right-click menu. Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc.

  1. Download the extension (.zip) and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  3. Pin it (the puzzle icon → pin), click it, and paste your connection code below.

Shortcuts once installed: Alt+Shift+S saves this tab, Alt+Shift+W saves the whole window.

Your connection code

It lets the extension save into your account. Treat it like a password; anyone with it can add faves (but can't read them).

Making a new code disconnects every browser that used the old one.

Faves Buddy on your phone

Open Faves Buddy in your phone's browser and sign in. Then:

  • Android: install it (Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen). It then appears in the share sheet: share a pile of Chrome tabs (tab switcher → Select tabs → ⋮ → Share tabs) or a batch of screenshots from Photos, and swipe through them here.
  • iPhone: Safari Share → Add to Home Screen. For tabs, use Safari's Copy N Links and paste them in Tidy my open tabs; for screenshots, pick a batch in Sweep my screenshots.
  • From a computer: the Chrome extension lists the tabs open on your phone's Chrome (with Chrome sync on) under Tabs on your other devices, ready to sort here.

AI with your own key

Faves Buddy sorts everything without AI. With your own Anthropic API key, it can also describe pictures and screenshots: what they show, the places and dates in them, a better title. The key is stored only in this browser and sent only to Anthropic; our server never sees it or your pictures' AI requests. You pay Anthropic directly (usually a fraction of a cent per picture).

Get a key at console.anthropic.com → API keys. Set a monthly spend limit there for peace of mind.

Dated events (Ticketmaster)

Discover finds festivals near you from Wikidata with no setup. For concerts, shows and games with dates, add a free Ticketmaster Discovery API key (5,000 searches a day). It stays in this browser and goes only to Ticketmaster.

  1. Sign up at developer.ticketmaster.com (free).
  2. Open “My Apps”: copy the Consumer Key.
  3. Paste it here.

Home spot

Used for distances, Discover and Top faves when your location isn't shared. Only the spot you choose is stored, never your live location.

Top faves

Top faves shows the places people save most around a spot. Your saved places count toward it anonymously: only places at least two people saved are shown, never who saved them, and never notes, tags or pictures.

What Faves Buddy learned from you

Each time you keep a fave in a category (or move it), the sorter remembers the website and the words in its title. No AI, no data leaves your account.
