Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Faves Buddy is free. The optional extras that cost money are paid by you directly to those services (an Anthropic API key for AI descriptions, usually a fraction of a cent per picture). Ticketmaster’s event key is free.

Is there an app for Android or iPhone?

Faves Buddy installs straight from your browser, no app store needed: on Android tap Install (or Chrome’s menu → Add to Home screen), on iPhone use Safari’s Share → Add to Home Screen. It then opens full screen like any app, and on Android it appears in your share menu. See Get the app for the steps.

Do you use AI to sort my things?

No. Sorting uses clear rules: what the page says about itself (schema.org, page type), the website, OpenStreetMap’s description of a place, and keywords in English and French. Then it learns from the choices you make. AI is optional, only for describing pictures, and only with your own key.

What happens to my screenshots?

They’re shrunk on your device and stored in your account. The text in them is read on your device with an open-source reader (Tesseract). Nothing goes to an AI unless you add your own key and ask for it.

Where do suggestions come from?

From open data: OpenStreetMap (places), Wikipedia (articles, places nearby) and Wikidata (festivals). With your own free key, Ticketmaster for dated events. “Top faves” counts the places Faves Buddy users save most around you, anonymously: only places at least two people saved, never who.

Do you track my location?

No. Your browser asks before sharing your location, and it’s only used on the page you’re on to show distances. If you choose a home spot, only that spot is saved.

Which browsers does the extension work in?

Chrome and Chromium browsers (Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera). On other browsers and phones, use the bookmarklet, paste links, or share screenshots into the app.

Can I get my data out?

Anytime, in four formats: JSON (everything), CSV, GeoJSON (your places) and a bookmarks file any browser can import.
