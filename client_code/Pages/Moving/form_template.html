Where to find your export

📥 Pocket

Pocket shut down in July 2025 and its export closed in October 2025. If you downloaded your export, unzip it and drop the .csv file inside (often part_000000.csv) into Import.

Kept: saved date, tags, archived items (they go to Archived, not your inbox).

💧 Raindrop.io

Settings → Backups (or Export) → CSV. Drop the file into Import.

Kept: saved date, folders as tags, tags, notes, highlights, excerpts, favourites (marked Loved).

📰 Instapaper

Settings → Export → Download .CSV file. Drop it into Import.

Kept: saved date, folders as tags, the text you selected, archived and starred items.

🗂️ OneTab, bookmarks, Google Maps

OneTab's export text, any browser's bookmarks file (HTML), or Google Takeout saved places. Folders become tags, places land on your map.
